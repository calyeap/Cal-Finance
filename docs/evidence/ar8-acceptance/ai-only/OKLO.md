## Is a conventional DCF decision-useful? No — **cannot conclude.**
Oklo has no operating history to base drivers on. It has essentially no revenue, has no NRC-licensed commercial reactor yet, and has not proven any unit economics: no realised capex per MW, capacity factor, PPA price or fuel (HALEU) cost.

In a 10-year FCFF model, all of the value would come from assumptions I would have to invent: the timing of first power, the build rate and the margin. A one-year slip, or a ±20% change in capex per MW, moves the result by multiples. Under the fixed method (11%, 3%), the output would be a projection of my own guesses, not a valuation. So I give **no fair value and no verdict.**
