Top risks
The draft's own Bear case (AI draft, every driver cited):
AI and cloud demand normalises faster than the current build-out assumes. Azure growth decelerates from the 43% posted in the June 2026 quarter toward the rate of the broader commercial base, Windows OEM, devices and Xbox keep shrinking, and revenue growth settles near the weakest year in the filed decade (6.9% in FY2023). The enormous capital programme — capex of $115.9bn in FY2026 against $34.3bn of depreciation — lands in the cost base as depreciation catches up, so operating margin gives back the recent gains and runs below the ten-year median rather than at the FY2026 peak of 46.8%. New capital earns roughly the discount rate: filed ROIC has already fallen from 94.0% in FY2018 to 26.0% in FY2026 as invested capital scaled, and the five-year incremental return of 17.3% compresses further in a capital-heavy AI regime. Rising stock-based compensation outpaces repurchases and share count drifts up slightly.
