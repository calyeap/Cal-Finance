The draft's own Bear case (AI draft, every driver cited):
The AI build-out that drove revenue from 60.9bn in FY2024 to 215.9bn in FY2026 proves to be a pull-forward: hyperscale customers digest the capacity they have bought, and NVIDIA's own filed history shows how fast this business can stall — revenue grew 0.2% in FY2023 and operating margin collapsed to 15.7% that year (H:revenue, H:margin). Growth flattens to low single digits off a very large base, pricing concessions and a heavier mix of lower-margin systems pull margin back toward the mid-30s, and the capital already deployed (invested capital rose from 45.4bn to 155.2bn in two years) earns far less than the 66-81% returns of the boom (H:returns). Stock compensation of 6.4bn a year keeps leaking share count (H:cashflow).
