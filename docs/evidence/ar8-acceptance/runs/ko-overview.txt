The draft's own Bear case (AI draft, every driver cited):
Coca-Cola's filed decade shows a top line that barely grew: 41.9bn in FY2016 against 47.9bn in FY2025, a 1.5% window CAGR, with outright declines in FY2017, FY2018 and FY2020 (H:revenue). The bear case assumes the recent step-up is mostly pricing that fades, volumes stay flat in developed markets, and currency and refranchising keep reported revenue growing about 2% a year. Margin gives back the FY2025 peak and settles near the FY2023-FY2024 zone, where impairment and legal charges pulled operating margin to 21.2% (H:margin). Net reinvestment runs above the filed median as capex continues its climb from 1,177m in FY2020 to 2,112m in FY2025 while D&A falls to 1,050m (H:capex), and each new dollar earns 10% \u2014 below the 9% discount rate plus moat credit, i.e. growth that barely pays for itself. Share count drifts up slightly with stock compensation of roughly 279m a year and no buyback disclosure in the supplied sources (H:cashflow).
